Tailor Health Method

Body measurements for health screening and clothes that fit. Screening flags, not diagnoses. For adults 18+; not for pregnancy or postpartum.

On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Safari clears website data after 7 days without use, and home-screen apps are exempt. Export a backup from the Data section.

Your measurements

Studies use sex-specific reference groups and none track non-binary people directly, so pick the baseline closest to your current endocrine and structural profile. Midpoint between lowest rib and top of hip bone, at the end of a relaxed exhale (optional: 3 slow 4-7-8 breaths first). Use a spring-loaded tape; measure twice and re-measure if they differ by more than 1 cm.

    Sources and limits
    

    What each result is based on
      

    • WHtR tiers (under 0.50, 0.50 to 0.59, 0.60+): Ashwell & Gibson, BMJ Open 2016; Ashwell, Gunn & Gibson, Obes Rev 2012. Less validated for adults 65+.
      • 

    • WHR: WHO Expert Consultation 2008. One cutoff per sex (0.90 men, 0.85 women), so two states.
      • 

    • TG/HDL 3.0 or more: McLaughlin et al., Ann Intern Med 2003. Derived in overweight, non-diabetic adults; sensitivity 64%, specificity 68%.
      • 

    • TyG: Simental-Mendía et al. 2008; Sánchez-García et al., Int J Endocrinol 2020 (cutoffs vary, so value only).
      • 

    • Neck 37/33 cm: Chinese cohort, Cardiovasc Diabetol 2013;12:76; Preis et al., JCEM 2010. Cutoffs vary by population.
      • 

    • Thigh under 60 cm: Heitmann & Frederiksen, BMJ 2009 (Danish cohort).
      • 

    • Arm 28.6/27.5 cm (age 65+): Clin Interv Aging 2021;16. Single study, needs validation.
      • 

    • Body fat: US Navy circumference equations and Jackson-Pollock/Siri skinfolds, standard published equations I did not re-check against the original papers. Individual error is about 4 points and larger in lean people, so no tiers are applied.
      • 

    • Bra: plus-4/plus-5 convention (Wacoal). The traditional method overestimates band size in about 76% and underestimates cup in about 84% of women, so treat it as a start. Sister sizes are a fitter convention.
      • 

    • Clothing charts below are placeholder sizes, not sourced. Replace them with brand charts.
      • 

    • Severe check: BMI under 15 is recognized as severe malnutrition in adults. The rapid-loss threshold (5% in 30 days) is a placeholder with no adult source.
      • 

    • Experimental ancestry notes are text only. No sourced numeric adjustments exist for WHtR.
